# STAR card (copy into each answer)

```markdown
## Situation
When, where, what broke, who felt it.

## Task
What you owned. Not the team. You.

## Action
Checks in order. What you refused. What you changed.

## Result
Number, time, or Git artifact. What you would repeat.
```

Rules:

- Situation is four sentences or fewer.
- Action is at least six sentences. This is the score.
- Result names a clock, a count, or a file.
- Do not name a current employer if the NDAs forbid it. ShopSphere lab names are fine.
